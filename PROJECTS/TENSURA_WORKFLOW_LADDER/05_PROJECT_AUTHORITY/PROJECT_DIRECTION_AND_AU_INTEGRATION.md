# Project Direction and AU Integration

**Project scope:** Volume 1 only for the current planning task.  
**Direction status:** User-defined working direction, recorded from explicit instructions.  
**Canon status:** No story canon has been approved.  
**Source verification:** Pending. Do not claim that the Volume 1 source has been fully read or reconciled.

## User-established premise
- A sixteen-year-old boy from our real Earth reincarnates through permanent fusion with Gobta.
- His Earth is different from the Earth Rimuru came from.
- This is fusion, not possession, takeover or replacement. Gobta's memories, instincts, experiences and identity remain part of the new person alongside the human's memories and personality.
- The human was intelligent and technically minded, with strong Minecraft Java Edition redstone/mechanism knowledge.
- Personality reference: Izayoi Sakamaki from *Problem Children Are Coming from Another World, Aren't They?*: confident, provocative, intelligent, competitive, playful and fearless.
- He begins as a goblin and is not physically Rimuru-level at the start.
- He openly tells Rimuru he reincarnated. Rimuru assumes they came from the same world; Gobta does not immediately correct him.
- Rimuru and Gobta develop a strong brotherly bond.

## Starting point and cave sequence
- Begin before the original Gobta scout group leaves to survey the forest and before the goblin village first meets Rimuru.
- Gobta deliberately goes to the sealed cave before the scout group.
- Gobta meets Rimuru and Veldora in the cave.
- Gobta asks Rimuru to name him first while Veldora is present. It is a genuine naming, not a nickname.
- Veldora names Rimuru afterward.
- Gobta and Rimuru leave the cave together. There is no second cave/forest encounter.

## Ability-system direction
- Working Unique Skill: **WORLD BUILDER**, formed from the human's final wishes to build, engineer, explore, create mechanisms and tame creatures.
- The system adapts Minecraft Java Edition-inspired mining, block placement, crafting, inventory, blueprints, a pocket dimension, experience, enchantments, mechanisms and taming to Tensura's magicule/skill framework.
- It is not unrestricted Creative Mode. Materials, knowledge, complexity, resistance, progression and costs matter.
- Living beings cannot simply be mined as resources; souls and Unique Skills cannot be copied by ordinary crafting.
- Minecraft-native creatures in the pocket dimension can be tamed. Tamed creatures brought into Tensura reality appear natural/organic rather than cube-shaped.
- External mining and terrain manipulation initially impose Minecraft-like block physics because Gobta has poor control. Later mastery lets him control how and where the skill applies those rules.
- Enchantments discovered or created through the skill's enchantment table may be applied beyond ordinary Minecraft item restrictions, subject to compatibility and mastery.
- Stored experience can eventually serve as Gobta's personal resource for naming monsters without drawing on his own magicule reserves. This is not free or unlimited and does not guarantee obedience.
- **Resonant Growth:** every major Rimuru power-up/evolution milestone triggers corresponding advancement in Gobta's own body, magicule capacity or WORLD BUILDER. This is not skill copying. Growth must be frequent and noticeable, but Gobta is not Rimuru's physical equal at the beginning.
- Mechanism-oriented Java/JavaScript-like configuration can develop earlier than unrestricted world commands. Unlimited item generation, mass-kill commands, Creative Mode and arbitrary world rewriting remain gated.
- Resonance Ore is a proposed AU mineral for magicule-responsive signal transmission/storage and redstone-like mechanisms. It is not canon Tensura material.

## User revision: double naming and second Unique Skill (2026-11-08)
- Gobta is named twice. Rimuru names him first, genuinely, and this substantially boosts/evolves his already-existing WORLD BUILDER.
- Veldora then gives Gobta the shared surname **Tempest**, the same surname as Rimuru and Veldora, as a deliberate brotherhood gift. Veldora sees Gobta as the weakest of the three and wants him included as family, not pitied.
- Veldora's naming awakens Gobta's second Unique Skill, proposed as **FORMULA ARCHITECT**: create original magic by designing formulas, but only to the extent Gobta truly understands the principles. It does not grant missing knowledge automatically.
- His Minecraft Java modding/coding background, experience designing mechanics/abilities, familiarity with modded magic systems and extensive manga reading make this restrictive skill a personal cheat. Knowledge is a head start, not automatic Tensura mastery.
- WORLD BUILDER and FORMULA ARCHITECT combine into magical engineering: design magical formulas, construct apparatus, enchant compatible components and configure mechanisms.
- The Dragon Slayer route remains: Gobta eventually develops his own Dragon Slayer Magic through study and formula creation, later has a Charybdis-related trigger/dragon transformation, and ultimately aims toward True Dragon evolution. All of this is future-volume direction, not Volume 1 accomplishment.

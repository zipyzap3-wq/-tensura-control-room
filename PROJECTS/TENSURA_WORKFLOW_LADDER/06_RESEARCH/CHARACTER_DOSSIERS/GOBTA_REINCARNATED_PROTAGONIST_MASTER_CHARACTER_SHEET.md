# GOBTA REINCARNATED PROTAGONIST | MASTER CHARACTER SHEET
**ID:** CHAR-GOBTA-REINCARNATED-01 | **Scope:** All-volume identity/progression; Volume 1 uses early-stage abilities only.  
**Status labels:** USER-DIRECTED / PROPOSED / OPEN / FUTURE / SOURCE-VERIFIED / CANON (only after explicit approval).

## 1. Identity and fused life
- **USER-DIRECTED:** A sixteen-year-old boy from our real Earth reincarnates into Gobta and permanently fuses with him. This is not possession, takeover, replacement or a human simply occupying Gobta's body.
- Gobta's memories, instincts, experiences, goblin identity and life remain part of the resulting new person. The human's memories/personality and Gobta's fuse; neither is erased.
- His Earth is distinct from Rimuru's. Human memories are meaningful, not vague, but do not grant omniscience about Tensura or guarantee remembered designs work in a magical world.
- Human background: technically minded; played Minecraft **Java Edition** and mods; understood coding/modding concepts; designed abilities/mechanics and their rules/behavior; read extensive manga with imaginative magic systems.
- Personality reference: Izayoi Sakamaki (*Problem Children Are Coming from Another World, Aren't They?*). Confident, provocative, intelligent, competitive, playful, fearless, challenges authority/powerful beings. Show through choices/dialogue/tactics/reactions, not narrator labels.
- Starts physically as Gobta, not instantly equal to Rimuru/high-tier monsters. Track appearance details in appearance/time-state files too.
- **Rimuru:** strong brotherly bond; both know human death/second life. Gobta openly says he reincarnated; Rimuru assumes same-world origin, Gobta doesn't immediately correct him. Rimuru doesn't discriminate against goblin Gobta and remains Volume 1's central protagonist/retains defining achievements.
- **Veldora:** proud, dramatic, boastful, theatrical, easily provoked, tsundere-like. Gobta's fearless teasing irritates him and can eventually entertain/attach him. Veldora deliberately treats all three as brothers; surname is recognition/gift, not pity.

## 2. Ability registry (SOLE authority for full ability descriptions)

### AB-01 | Unique Skill: WORLD BUILDER
**Status:** USER-DIRECTED core; post-naming evolution title OPEN.  
**Origin:** Exists through reincarnation/final wishes and Tensura skill-acquisition logic. Expresses creation, construction, engineering, exploration, systems, resources and creature taming, not merely strength.

**Initial capability families (concept fixed; onset/costs OPEN):**
- Minecraft Java-inspired mining, compatible block/resource breaking, placement, construction, crafting, blueprint recording, spatial inventory/storage, pocket dimension, experience progression, enchantments, mechanisms and creature taming.
- Pocket dimension is a genuine world environment with terrain, resources, mobs, farms and mechanisms, not just storage.
- Pocket-world creatures can be tamed; compatible Tensura monsters may eventually be tamed. Creatures brought into Tensura appear natural/organic rather than cubic; blocks/infrastructure/skill-mediated terrain may retain block form.
- Early external mining/chopping/digging imposes Minecraft-like block physics imperfectly. Precision improves with mastery; no full-world toggle at start.
- Java-oriented mechanism design/configuration/coding knowledge is translated into a skill interface, not literal Minecraft software in Tensura.
- Enchantments can eventually apply to compatible objects, machinery, equipment and structures beyond strict vanilla restrictions, subject to compatibility, cost, stability and mastery.
- **Not Creative Mode:** no free resources, arbitrary give/kill-all commands, unlimited edits or automatic soul/Unique Skill copying. Resource/material resistance, magicule, complexity, knowledge and progression remain constraints.
- Does not replace Tensura magic, skills, resistances, martial development or ordinary learning.

**Rimuru naming upgrade (USER-DIRECTED):**
- Rimuru genuinely names Gobta first. Real naming with magicule/body/evolution consequences, not nickname.
- Substantially strengthens/improves WORLD BUILDER capabilities, precision and magical compatibility. No second Unique Skill from this event.
- Exact evolved title, quantitative scale and precise unlocked functions remain OPEN. Do not invent final evolution name.

**Registered Blueprint / Instant Creation (USER-DIRECTED, 2026-11-08):**
- After Gobta successfully creates/completes a construction or machine, WORLD BUILDER can register its blueprint.
- He can then instantly recreate that registered design elsewhere, deploying a saved construction instead of manually rebuilding it.
- All required materials/components must be on hand in inventory/storage and are consumed. No free materials, missing-part substitution or cost bypass.
- Destination needs sufficient space and must satisfy placement/occupancy constraints. Deployment doesn't automatically solve targeting, energy, ammunition, activation, control, firing or safety.
- An imagined, incomplete or untested design cannot be instant-created; first construction must exist and be completed/registered.
- User example: eventual orbital strike cannon. After building/registering it and carrying the recipe materials, Gobta can instantly recreate it wherever he is. **FUTURE example, not Volume 1 feat.**
- This makes portable logistics, gathering and shulker-box storage major progression goals.

**Inventory and storage (USER-DIRECTED):**
- Dedicated Minecraft-style inventory works in Tensura world as well as pocket dimension; access isn't restricted to pocket world.
- He doesn't want ordinary slots clogged by all construction materials; shulker boxes are key logistics/storage.
- Goal: beat vanilla Minecraft, reach End, explore End Cities and collect shulker shells/boxes to expand carrying capacity.
- Tensura is his stronger growth environment: power up there, then return to Minecraft to overcome vanilla progression/bosses and gather resources. Not conquered at start.
- Inventory size, nested-container rules, weight/volume, cross-world persistence and exact consumption are OPEN. Do not silently grant infinite recursive storage.

**Experience and personal naming (USER-DIRECTED concept; mechanics OPEN):**
- Minecraft-like experience from qualifying combat, farming, gathering, crafting, construction and meaningful skill activity.
- Gobta may spend experience to name compatible monsters. This is his mechanic, not ordinary Tensura naming.
- Target strength/soul/compatibility/cost constrain it. Naming doesn't automatically create loyalty or taming.
- Does not replace Rimuru's genuine naming of Gobta.

**Resonant Growth (USER-DIRECTED concept; scale OPEN):**
- Because Rimuru named Gobta and they share a meaningful soul connection, major Rimuru progression/evolution milestones trigger corresponding Gobta growth: WORLD BUILDER advancement, body, magicule capacity or unlocks.
- Not copying Rimuru's skills/resistances. Gobta's system grows in resonance.
- Enables fast meaningful growth while preserving initial power gap.

### AB-02 | Unique Skill: FORMULA ARCHITECT
**Status:** PROPOSED name/design; Veldora naming awakens second skill as user-directed event. Name/functions not finalized.
- Veldora names Rimuru and Gobta with shared surname **Tempest**; deliberately gifts Gobta the surname as family/brotherhood recognition despite perceiving him as weakest brother. Three are brothers. Veldora stays dramatic/proud, not out-of-character sentimental.
- Veldora's naming awakens Gobta's second Unique Skill. Exact order/mechanics source-check before drafting; emotional intent fixed.
- **Core (USER-DIRECTED):** create original magic by designing magical formulas, not merely learning/copying spells.
- Can potentially analyze, construct, modify, combine, optimize, store and theoretically test formulas, but does not supply missing knowledge.
- **Knowledge gate:** Gobta genuinely understands principles used: magicules, magical theory, physics, energy behavior, interactions, mechanisms and conditions. Without understanding, skill cannot conjure it; for most people it may be nearly useless.
- His technical background, Minecraft modding/coding, ability-design practice, manga/magic-system familiarity, imagination, experiments and World Builder facilities make it a cheat in his hands.
- Fictional ability familiarity is inspiration, not proof of Tensura principle knowledge. Translate, study, test, experiment, use resources/magicules and account for compatibility/failure.
- **Proposed functions (not locked):** Formula Analysis, Construction, Modification, Combination, Optimization, Storage and Theory/Experiment Simulation. Simulation depends on accurate inputs/understanding; not prophecy or guaranteed safety.
- Knowledge, time, resources, magicules, complexity, execution, compatibility and failure risk remain. Cheat factor comes from his knowledge/creativity and skill synergy, not omnipotence.

## Character-state dossiers (mandatory)\n\nEach recurring or consequential character gets a dedicated state dossier, separate from Gobta's master sheet. Use a stable file per character, e.g. \\`CHARACTER_STATES/RIMURU_TEMPEST.md\\`, not one enormous unsearchable document. Link it from this index. Maintain a master index and chapter update queue.\n\n### Required fields in every character-state dossier\n- Identity, aliases/titles, species, age/time-state where relevant.\n- Appearance at the current story point, including changes/injuries/clothing when continuity matters.\n- Personality, attitude, speech/behavior patterns, values, motives and current emotional state.\n- Current powers, skills, resistances, equipment, demonstrated feats, limits, weaknesses and what remains unknown. Use canon/source verification and story-time accuracy; never give future-volume abilities early.\n- Relationships and individual opinions: how this character views Gobta (the fused reincarnated protagonist, not baseline Gobta), Rimuru and relevant others; trust, fear, rivalry, affection, suspicion, respect, resentment, etc. Record why and what caused it.\n- Knowledge/beliefs: what they know about Gobta, what they misunderstand, secrets, evidence/source and when learned. Keep inference distinct from fact.\n- **Last interaction memory:** date/chapter/scene, what happened, exact consequential words/actions, what this character remembers, emotional takeaway, unresolved promises/conflicts and likely recollection triggers.\n- Relationship progression: previous stage → current stage, trigger/evidence, direction and unresolved tension.\n- Chapter update history with chapter ID, approved status and changes.\n\n## End-of-chapter update gate (mandatory)\n1. During drafting/review, maintain a provisional update list for every character who appears, is discussed consequentially, learns something, changes power/state, or has a relationship/attitude affected. Include off-screen characters only when the chapter changes what they know or how they view someone.\n2. On chapter approval, update each affected character-state dossier: appearance/state, powers/feats/limits, attitude/opinion toward Gobta and others, knowledge/beliefs, last-interaction memory, relationship progression, and continuity facts. Mark unchanged fields as unchanged when relevant; do not fabricate developments.\n3. Update the character index, knowledge/relationship ledgers and continuity ledger as needed; record the chapter as the evidence/source for each change.\n4. These updates are part of approval completion, not optional cleanup. No approval means no permanent character-state update; keep it provisional.\n5. Next chapter planning must consult these dossiers before writing dialogue, reactions, callbacks, recollections or power displays.\n\n## Placement and authority rules
- **Master character sheet:** identity, personality, appearance reference, ability registry, full capability/limit rules, progression roadmap, user ideas and unresolved decisions.
- **Time-state cards:** what the character can actually do/know/look like at a particular story time.
- **Knowledge matrix:** what each character knows, source and acquisition timing.
- **Arc plans:** plot beats and ability-ID references, never duplicate full ability descriptions.
- **Chapter packets:** scene-local capability state and necessary consequences; link the master sheet.
- **Continuity ledgers:** changes after scenes, not master design.

## Canon route and dialogue preservation\n- Source chunks are followed in strict order. A custom bridge chapter may be inserted only between the completed endpoint of one chunk and the beginning of its immediate next chunk. It must not jump/reorder source chunks. Record both anchors.\n- Dialogue that still fits the altered circumstances, character knowledge and scene logic should remain unchanged. Change/add dialogue only when Gobta/AU circumstances, causality, character voice or continuity require it. Preserve source provenance and note meaningful adaptations; do not rewrite lines just for novelty.\n\n## Next action
Use the master sheet as the only authoritative ability/growth reference. Keep index and Workflow Ladder updated as dossiers are added or moved.

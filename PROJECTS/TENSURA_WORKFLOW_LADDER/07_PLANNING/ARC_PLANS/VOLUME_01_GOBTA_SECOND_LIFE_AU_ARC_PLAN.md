# VOLUME 1 ARC PLAN
## Gobta: The Second Life / World Builder AU

**Plan status:** User-defined working plan. Not source-verified. Not story canon.  
**Scope:** Volume 1 only. Do not expand this planning task into Volumes 2–7.  
**Protagonist:** A sixteen-year-old boy from our real Earth, permanently fused with Gobta.  
**Core promise:** A technically minded, confident reincarnator with a Minecraft Java Edition-inspired Unique Skill grows alongside Rimuru, builds a new form of goblin engineering, and becomes Rimuru's brother and partner without taking Rimuru's central Volume 1 achievements away from him.

---

## 1. Project premise and protagonist

### 1.1 Reincarnation and fusion
- The protagonist is a sixteen-year-old boy from our real Earth. His Earth is distinct from the Earth Rimuru came from.
- He reincarnates by permanently fusing with Gobta. This is not possession, takeover, replacement, or a human mind simply occupying Gobta's body.
- The result is a new person carrying both Gobta's memories, instincts, experiences and goblin identity, and the human's memories, personality and technical knowledge.
- Gobta is not erased. His life and identity remain part of the fused person.
- The human was intelligent and technically minded, with particular skill and enthusiasm for designing Minecraft redstone mechanisms.
- His favorite game was Minecraft Java Edition. His memories are meaningful knowledge, not vague impressions, but they do not grant omniscience about Tensura or guarantee every remembered design will work in a magical world.

### 1.2 Personality direction
- Personality reference: Izayoi Sakamaki from *Problem Children Are Coming from Another World, Aren't They?*
- He is confident, provocative, intelligent, competitive, playful and fearless, and willing to challenge powerful people.
- He is not needlessly submissive to authority and does not become timid merely because someone is stronger.
- His confidence must be expressed through decisions, dialogue, tactical thinking and reactions, not narration merely claiming he is confident.
- He begins as a goblin. His initial physical strength is not equal to Rimuru's.

### 1.3 Relationship with Rimuru
- He and Rimuru develop a strong brotherly bond.
- Both carry memories of tragic human deaths and lives from another world.
- The protagonist openly tells Rimuru that he reincarnated.
- Rimuru assumes they came from the same world. The protagonist does not correct this assumption immediately because he has no urgent reason to complicate the conversation.
- Rimuru does not discriminate against him for being a goblin.
- Rimuru remains the central protagonist of the original Volume 1 story and retains his defining achievements and emotional arc.

### 1.4 Relationship with Veldora
- Veldora remains recognizable: proud, theatrical, boastful, dramatic, easily provoked and capable of becoming entertained or attached despite himself.
- The protagonist is not afraid of Veldora and is willing to tease or challenge him.
- Their exchanges must grow from what was actually said and done in the scene. No random interjections or disconnected banter.
- Veldora's reactions should follow his established personality rather than reducing him to a generic comic sidekick.

- The story begins before the original Gobta scout group leaves to survey the forest and before the goblin village's first encounter with Rimuru.
- The protagonist recognizes the approaching opportunity and deliberately plans to reach the sealed cave before the scout group.
- He enters the cave and meets Rimuru and Veldora there.
- This is the single cave encounter. Gobta and Rimuru leave the cave together.
- Do not split this into a cave meeting followed by a second, separate forest meeting. Do not make the characters inexplicably encounter one another twice.
- While Veldora is present, the protagonist deliberately asks Rimuru to name him before Veldora names Rimuru.
- Rimuru's act must be a genuine Tensura-style naming, not a nickname or casual label. The naming has real consequences and must be reconciled with the world's naming and magicule rules.
- After Rimuru names Gobta, Veldora names Rimuru. Preserve the intended order.
- The cave dialogue must be fluent, context-sensitive and faithful to the characters' conversational logic and source personalities. The AU character is integrated into the scene rather than pasted into it as unrelated quips.

### Final Chapter: The Goblin Who Inherited a World

- Show the village's evolution, the craftsmen's presence, Gobta's workshop and a meaningful demonstration of WORLD BUILDER/pocket-dimension development.
- End at the original Volume 1 destination and preserve its major emotional and narrative outcomes.

## 10. Resonance Ore: AU engineering material

**Status:** AU invention, not a canon Tensura mineral.

Working concept: a mineral/material that responds to magicule flow and can be processed into a redstone-like signal and control medium.

Proposed properties:
- Responds to magicule flow.
- Can maintain or transmit shaped magical signals.
- Can store a small charge.
- Responds to pulses and patterns.
- Can operate switches, traps and simple automated mechanisms.
- Requires processing and refinement to prevent leakage or signal instability.
- Can be developed into a foundation for more complex magical engineering.

Use canon magic ore, magisteel and other established materials as context where appropriate, but never describe Resonance Ore itself as established canon.

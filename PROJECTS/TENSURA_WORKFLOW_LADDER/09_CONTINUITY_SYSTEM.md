# Continuity System
## Core ledgers
- Timeline: date/time, source event, project event, duration, location, sequence confidence.
- Character state: condition, appearance, clothing, abilities, resources, emotional state, goals, knowledge and limits.
- Knowledge: who knows what, when/how learned, confidence, misconceptions and secrets.
- Relationships: trust, obligations, conflict, leverage, boundaries and changes.
- Open threads: setup, owner, last touched, intended payoff, status and next opportunity.
- Source crosswalk: volume/chapter/page/chunk mapped to project scenes and divergence.
- Conflict register: contradictory readings, canon conflicts, unresolved questions and authority.

## Temporal discipline
Dossiers are time-appropriate. Later abilities, revelations, relationships or knowledge cannot leak into earlier scenes. Track what characters do not yet know.

## Update rules
Draft observations are DRAFT ONLY and do not change canon ledgers. After user approval and canon promotion, update affected ledgers with the approved chapter/decision as evidence. Preserve prior states and history.

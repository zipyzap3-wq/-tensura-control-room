# Authoring Preferences
These are craft preferences, not plot or character canon:
- Make the story feel alive through active characters, consequential choices, named places and specific physical staging.
- Prefer dialogue, monologue and action over excessive narration. Use moderate, purposeful monologues rather than long exposition blocks.
- Give characters clear introductions and meaningful reactions to important changes.
- Use deliberate POV switches when they add meaningful information or emotional perspective; do not drift between minds.
- Read ahead in source material before drafting so additions integrate with upcoming canon events.
- Avoid unnamed or vague transitions. Track locations, movement, time and scene-to-scene continuity.
- Use narrative maps and physical geography where they materially clarify the setting, movement or tactical scene.
- Keep character reactions credible without weakening their competence or flattening their personalities.
- Do not reveal secret or future knowledge prematurely when doing so would be operationally irrational for the character.
- Avoid cheap, bland or mechanically compressed scenes. Preserve meaningful source beats while improving execution.
- End planning at major unresolved canon decisions and let the user choose/approve those decisions.
- Take creative initiative in proposing scene execution and solutions, but do not invent user-approved canon or make protected decisions on the user's behalf.

These preferences may be refined by later explicit user instructions. Do not use them as permission to import the old story's protagonist, AU, skills, relationships or plot.

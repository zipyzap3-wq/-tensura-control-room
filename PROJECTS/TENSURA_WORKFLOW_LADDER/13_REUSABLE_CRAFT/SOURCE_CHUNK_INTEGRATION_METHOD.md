# Source-Chunk Integration Method
## Mandatory operating companion
Before processing any source chunk, use `CHUNK_PROCESSING_AND_HANDOFF_PROTOCOL.md` (TWL-021). That protocol controls queue order, full-reading states, extraction records, cross-chunk carryover, reconciliation, verification and live handoff. This file controls how verified source information is adapted faithfully. Both are required.

## Core principle: source as event track, adaptation as a new layer
The source controls the order and substance of canonical events within its verified range. The adaptation may add viewpoint, dialogue, reactions, tactics, consequences, transitions and emotional texture, but additions must fit around the source rather than replace, contradict or pre-empt it. Preserve major event order, causal logic, participants, demonstrations and outcomes unless a divergence is explicitly approved.

## Before drafting: exact source accounting
1. Identify exact volume, chapter, file, page range, start beat and stop beat.
2. Read the assigned source range in full.
3. Read ahead beyond the intended chapter endpoint to understand setup, foreshadowing, later consequences and the next unadapted event.
4. Read relevant prior approved project chapters and current continuity records.
5. Build an ordered beat ledger. Each beat records source location, event/action, participants, cause, information revealed, reaction, consequence and adaptation status.
6. Build a cast ledger: every named participant, entrance/exit, role, responsibility and who is present for each scene.
7. Mark source facts, inference, unknowns, approved divergence and proposed invention separately.
8. Identify chunk boundaries that cut across scenes. Split or combine reading units when necessary to preserve complete scene logic; never let file boundaries dictate artificial chapter breaks.

## Three levels of creative freedom
- **Level 1, faithful enrichment:** improve dialogue, sensory grounding, reactions, tactical clarity, internal thought, pacing and transitions without changing event substance.
- **Level 2, compatible connective additions:** add brief scenes, interactions or viewpoint passages that fit the source sequence, do not steal another character's responsibility and do not reveal future information.
- **Level 3, structural divergence:** change outcomes, timing, participant responsibility, reveal order or major causal events. This requires explicit user approval and must be recorded as a proposed or approved divergence.

## Anti-compression and scene coverage
Do not reduce a complex source sequence to a few summary paragraphs. Preserve meaningful interactions, reactions, demonstrations, jokes, world details, tactical beats, transitions and scene purposes. A chapter may reorganize sentence-level presentation but must not accidentally omit source substance. Do not claim a percentage of retained content unless it was actually assessed.

## Named cast and responsibility lock
Do not invent unnamed characters as if source-confirmed. Do not omit named participants who materially affect the scene. Track who performs each action, who witnesses it, who arrives/departs, and who is responsible for the outcome. An adaptation must not transfer a canonical character's achievement to another character without an approved divergence.

## Post-draft fidelity audit
Compare draft against source beat by beat:
- all important events retained in order;
- causality and outcomes preserved or divergence logged;
- cast and responsibility correct;
- dialogue and reactions adapted rather than mechanically copied;
- no future knowledge leaked;
- no source detail omitted merely because the draft is shorter;
- next unadapted source beat and handoff recorded.

## Hard gate
No source verification, no drafting. Required artifacts: exact source inventory, event/beat ledger, cast ledger, explicit divergence overlay, post-draft source audit and handoff. If a source conflict cannot be resolved, record it and stop the affected scene rather than inventing an answer.


## Required living handoff
After each source range, update the source read log, coverage matrix, beat/cast ledgers and execution checkpoint. The next pointer must identify the exact next source file and action. A source range is not complete until TWL-021 marks it VERIFIED.

## Reconciliation rules
1. Control Room Ladder governs overall stages, evidence, file architecture, source intake and handoff.
2. Gobta Master Workflow governs story-specific execution and draft lifecycle.
3. Satisfy both; do not duplicate evidence unnecessarily.
4. Map stages by purpose if numbering differs.
5. Conflicts are blockers. Do not silently choose or edit either authority.
6. Source canon is research input, not AU canon.
7. A source read counts only when full text and identity are verified and evidence recorded.
8. GitHub-first draft remains the story workflow. Gemini does not silently become the draft source of truth.
